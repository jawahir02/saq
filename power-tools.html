Our Partner Brands

DeWalt

One of the oldest power tools brands in the world.

A top choice globally for professionals in concrete and carpentry. Known for inventing the portable electric drill, DeWalt offers a massive range of corded and cordless options. Their innovative FlexVolt battery technology powers almost any heavy-duty equipment on site.

Stanley

Excellence in construction since 1843.

Setting the standard in the construction industry, Stanley manufactures superior quality power tools that help professionals build, repair, and protect. They continue a long legacy of innovation and strength.

Clarke

Best after-sales services and cost-effective solutions.

Clarke supplies a vast range of electrical and mechanical equipment, including drills, cut-off saws, and impact drivers. They stand out from competitors by offering exceptional after-sales support and reliable warranties.

Bosch

A familiar name amongst contractors since 1886.

Appreciated for quality, sustainability, and robust performance. Bosch offers an innovative portfolio of cordless tools and measuring devices. Known for unmatched battery reliability and flexibility, Bosch tools help pros do the job perfectly.

Makita

High quality and extremely powerful since 1958.

Makita tools are a staple on construction sites, offering a streamlined cordless system with powerful voltage options. From circular saws to impact drivers, their heavy-duty equipment is trusted by true professionals for substantial building renovations.
