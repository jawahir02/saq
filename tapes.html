Product Categories

Double Sided & Masking Tape

Easy-to-tear, pressure-sensitive adhesive masking tapes.

Available in a variety of widths, our masking tapes (or sticky tapes) are designed primarily for painting to cleanly mask off areas without leaving residue or causing damage. We offer tapes in various adhesion strengths, including custom orders for specific industrial or household applications.

BOPP Tapes

Solvent-free, pressure-sensitive adhesive tapes for packaging.

BOPP tapes adhere with application pressure and require no water or heat for activation. They consist of a pressure-sensitive adhesive coated onto a backing material like plastic film. We supply both single-sided for bonding to surfaces and double-sided tapes with removable release liners.

Tile Protection Sheet

High-quality floor protection meeting international standards.

We supply top-quality tile protection sheets designed for rigid construction and ease of use. Our supervisors oversee every step of product development to ensure unmatched quality at economical prices, keeping your expensive flooring safe during construction works.
