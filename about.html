About

LEADING BUILDING MATERIALS SUPPLIER IN DUBAI

Established in 2008 as an authorized Jotun dealer, SAQ Building Materials has spent nearly two decades supporting contractors, designers, and builders across Dubai and the UAE.

We understand that modern projects demand more than just standard supplies—they require precision, quality, and flexibility.

Beyond our core expertise in premium Jotun paints and protective coatings, we specialize in supplying a comprehensive range of materials tailored specifically for interior design and fit-out works.

Whether you need exact finishes for a high-end interior space or dependable site deliveries without frustrating minimum order restrictions, we make sure you get the right products exactly when you need them.
