Product Categories

Bituminous Membrane

We supply top-quality bituminous waterproofing products from Weber Sodamco and Henkel Polybit. These sheet membranes and paints are highly flexible, offer shape stability, accommodate structural movement, and provide a positive barrier to water and vapour while resisting atmospheric agents and chemical attacks.

Cement Based Waterproofing

Products like weber dry 110 FX form an elastomeric, resistant membrane suitable for potable water tanks, reservoirs, canals, and tunnels. It offers excellent adhesion on damp substrates, resists high hydrostatic positive and negative pressures, and prevents reinforcement corrosion while remaining non-toxic and chloride-free.

Polyurethane (PU) Liquid Membranes

PU membranes create a continuous watertight seal with permanent high elasticity and UV resistance. They are easy to apply via brush, roller, or spray gun and provide long-lasting waterproofing with excellent adhesion to concrete, metal, bitumen membranes, and acrylic paints. Ideal for water storage tanks, flat rooftops, and flowerbeds.

PU Joint Sealant

Our PU construction joint sealants (like weber joint seal) cure under atmospheric humidity to form highly flexible, tear-resistant joints. They offer excellent adhesion on most construction materials, resist UV rays and climatic ageing, do not stain marble or granite, and can be easily painted over after full curing.
