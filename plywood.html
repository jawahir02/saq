Product Types

Marine / Construction Plywood

Durable, moisture-resistant plywood sourced from China, Russia & Malaysia.

Manufactured from tropical hardwood veneers with negligible core gaps and Water & Boil Proof (WBP) exterior glue, marine plywood resists delamination and fungal attack — ideal for humid and wet environments including formwork and shuttering.

  • Formwork & moulds for wet concrete
  • Flooring systems
  • Shutters for concreting

Commercial Plywood

Engineered wood from three or more glued plies — 1 to 4mm thick sheets.

Made by peeling steam-treated logs into thin plies that are bonded together into flat sheets. Commercial plywood is versatile, cost-effective, and ideal for indoor applications requiring a stable, smooth surface.

  • Partition walls
  • Furniture, cupboards & kitchen cabinets
  • Light doors and shutters

White Wood / Soft Wood

Economical softwood plywood sourced from Romania, Germany & Austria.

White wood accounts for 80% of all timber globally and offers a wide range of building applications. Available in various grades and dimensions, this softwood is significantly less expensive than hardwood while still providing excellent structural support.

  • Supports for partition walls
  • Shuttering supports for concrete formwork
  • Light doors and shutters

MDF

High-density engineered wood panels sourced from China & Malaysia.

Medium-density fibreboard (MDF) is produced by compressing wood fibres under high temperature and pressure, resulting in a denser, stronger panel than particle board. Its smooth surface makes it ideal for furniture and decorative indoor applications.

  • Furniture, cupboards & kitchen cabinets
  • Tile & marble protection films
  • Indoor finishing (not suitable for wet areas)
