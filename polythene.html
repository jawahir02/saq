Polythene Sheet Supplier in Dubai

SAQ Building Materials is a leading supplier of highly versatile polythene sheets in Dubai, UAE, made from premium synthetic polyethylene resin.

Our heavy-duty polythene sheet rolls offer the ultimate plastic protection, customized to suit the specific length, width, and weight requirements of any project.

Key Features & Applications

  • Waterproofing: Acts as a vapour and moisture barrier in construction foundations and concrete slabs.
  • Damp-Proof Membrane: Ideal for severe dampness conditions (recommended 1000 gauge minimum).
  • Protection: Shields flooring, construction supplies, and structures from high pressure and harsh weather.
  • Versatile Use: Widely applicable in painting, agriculture, greenhouse, and nursery environments.

Concrete Site Installation

  • Base Layer: Laid on a blinding layer of sand or compacted fuel ash over the hardcore surface.
  • Lapping: Lapped 150mm at joints and continued across surrounding walls under the DPC.
  • Sealing: Overlap joints are sealed with mastic tape and completed with polythene jointing tape.
  • Specifications: Standard rolls are 4m x 25m (~1kg/m). Custom sizing available.
